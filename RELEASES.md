# V1.2.0 (07/10/2026)

- September Game Creators Club release
- Sound effects across the game
- Balance tweaks: a day lasts 7 minutes instead of 5, and the painting is less harsh on paint over the lines or outside the model

# V1.1.0 (05/10/2026)

- Painting minigame: timed brush painting with three brush sizes, scored on how well each area is covered and how little goes over the lines
- Coding minigame: time limit, a clearer caret and live stars
- Prompt before each minigame starts, showing what to use (keyboard, mouse)
- Profit bar on the launch results, against the day's target
- The skin is built progressively, with a cutscene after each station
